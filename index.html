01 — About Snapshot

Who We Are

San Marin Speech & Debate started as a handful of students who just wanted to argue, and somehow turned that into something real. No professional setup, no outside help. Just a group of kids who figured it out together, year after year.

Over a decade later, the team has won competitions across the Bay Area, made it to the California State Tournament, and in 2026 walked into one of the biggest invitationals in the state and finished in the top eight. Twenty-six students. One volunteer coach. And the thing is, that almost didn't happen...

03 — How It Works

Parliamentary Debate, Made Simple

Two teams get a topic shortly before the round, then trade six speeches — one side for, one side against. No scripts, no memorizing. Everyone starts at Novice, and we teach the rest at practice.
