01 · About Snapshot

Who We Are

San Marin Speech & Debate started as a handful of students who just wanted to argue, and somehow turned that into something real. No professional setup, no outside help. Just a group of kids who figured it out together, year after year.

Nearly twenty years later, the team has won competitions across the Bay Area, made it to the California State Tournament, and in 2026 walked into one of the biggest invitationals in the state and finished in the top eight. Twenty-six students. One volunteer coach. And the thing is, that almost didn't happen...

03 · How It Works

Parliamentary Debate, Made Simple

Two teams get a topic shortly before the round, then trade six speeches, one side for and one side against. No scripts, no memorizing. Everyone starts at Novice, and we teach the rest at practice.

Start Here Novice
Starting off

Longer prep time and shorter speeches, so there is room to find your footing.

JV
Getting there

Full-length speeches against more experienced debaters.

Varsity
The top level

Our most experienced competitors, at the highest level of competition.

Same format at every level. Four constructive speeches, then two rebuttals. Only the clock changes. Each block below is one speech, sized to its minutes.

Our Foundation of Generosity & Support

San Marin Speech and Debate does not run on its own. Our growth, our results, and how far we can travel to compete all rest on people who chose to invest in a student program.

Our squad has grown to 30+ active members

A bigger team is a good problem, but it is still a problem. Registration, travel, and lodging all scale with the number of students we bring, and we compete against private programs with budgets we do not have. Grants are what close that gap.
