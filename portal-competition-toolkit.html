Tournament Packing List

Section 3

Everything you need to bring on tournament day. No excuses.

0 / 14 packed All Packed
"Dress professionally. You're representing San Marin."
Boys: Dress slacks with a nicer shirt and a tie if you have one. A full suit is even better.
Girls: Pants or skirts (not too short) with blouses, sweaters, or blazers. Lean toward the professional side.
  • Try to have a little color; avoid being completely black and white.
  • Everything cleaned and ironed.
  • Comfortable shoes or bring a pair to change into.
  • Don't forget deodorant.
  • If rain is predicted, bring rain gear.
