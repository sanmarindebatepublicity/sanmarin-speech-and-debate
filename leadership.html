How We Operate

Program Structure

Executive Leadership
Presidents & Vice Presidents
  • Lead the team and set the direction for the season
  • Coordinate tournaments, travel logistics, and schedules
  • Manage communications to members and the school community
  • Oversee finances, league registration, and fundraising
  • Secure grants and funding for club operations
Team Captains
Debate & Publicity
  • Plan and run weekly practices for all skill levels
  • Develop curriculum and drill competitive arguments
  • Mentor novice debaters through their first tournaments
  • Manage social media presence and outreach campaigns
  • Promote the team at school events and to incoming students
Faculty & Advisors
Coach & Faculty Advisor
  • Provide expert coaching on strategy, evidence, and delivery
  • Guide debaters through varsity and JV competition circuits
  • Serve as the official school-sanctioned faculty sponsor
  • Liaise with administration on club activities and travel
  • Ensure compliance with school and league policies
Message from Leadership

Logan Alsterlind, Co-Vice President
